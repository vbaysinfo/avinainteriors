## Video style
- Vertical 9:16 for Reels/Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube.
- First 3 seconds: the best "wow" shot or the finished result.
- Calm, premium background music. Avoid loud trending audio unless the owner
  approves.
- End card: logo + "WhatsApp: [NUMBER]" + "Free consultation".

## Do
- Show real projects, real factory, real team.
- Mention the area (e.g. "3BHK in Madhurawada") when the customer agrees.
- Explain materials simply (BWP vs HDHMR, laminate vs acrylic).

## Don't
- Don't show customer faces, names, flat numbers or car plates without consent.
- Don't compare with or criticise competitors by name.
- Don't promise prices, dates or discounts in content that the owner hasn't
  approved.
- Don't use stock photos as if they are our work.

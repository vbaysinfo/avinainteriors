# Vbays Phase 0: Start Marketing Today (no third-party tools)

**Your decision:** no Buffer, no Upload-Post, no other middlemen. Vbays
itself will post to Instagram and YouTube through their **official APIs**
(Meta and Google). That is built in **Phase 2**.

Until then, Phase 0 gives you the "brain" right away: Claude writes your
content, and you post it yourself for the next few weeks.

```
TODAY (Phase 0)                        FROM PHASE 2 (fully automatic)
────────────────                       ───────────────────────────────
You ─chat─▶ Claude Desktop Project     Vbays plans the week, picks your real
           writes Reels, captions,     photos from Google Drive, makes the
           hashtags, YouTube SEO       video, sends it to your Telegram
You post it on Instagram/YouTube       → you tap ✅ → Vbays posts it itself
                                       on Instagram + YouTube at the best time
```

The only outside companies involved are the platforms themselves: Meta
(Instagram, WhatsApp), Google (YouTube, Drive, Calendar), Telegram, and
Anthropic (Claude).

---

## Step 1: Create the Claude Project (10 min)

1. Install **Claude Desktop** from <https://claude.ai/download> and sign in.
2. Left sidebar → **Projects** → **Create project** → name it
   `Vbays — Avina Interiors`.
3. **Project instructions:** open
   `vbays/phase0/CLAUDE_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, copy everything **below the
   line**, paste it in, and save.
4. **Project knowledge** → **+ Add content** → upload the 5 files in
   `vbays/knowledge/`.
5. **Before uploading**, fill every `[SQUARE BRACKET]` and check every
   `(confirm)`. Claude will only say what these files say.

✅ **Test it:** in a chat inside the Project, type:
> Plan this week's Instagram and YouTube content. Telugu-English mix.

## Step 2: Get your accounts ready for Phase 2 (do it now, it takes time)

These are needed for Vbays to post by itself. Meta and Google checks can
take days, so start early.

1. **Instagram → Business account:** Instagram app → Profile → ☰ →
   *Account type and tools* → *Switch to professional account* → **Business**.
2. **Facebook Page:** make sure Avina Interiors has a Facebook Page and you
   are its admin. Link it: Instagram → *Edit profile* → *Page*.
3. **Meta developer account:** sign up at <https://developers.facebook.com>
   with the same Facebook login. (The same account is used for WhatsApp in
   Phase 3.)
4. **Google account** that owns the `@avinainteriors` YouTube channel. Keep
   its login ready.
5. **Business verification (Meta):** have your GST certificate or business
   registration handy. Meta asks for it for WhatsApp Business.

## Step 3: Daily routine until Phase 2 (10 min a day)

1. **Monday:** "Plan this week." Adjust the plan and reply "approved".
2. **Each day:** attach 3–10 real project or factory photos/videos:
   > Make today's Reel from these. Telugu-English mix.
3. Copy the caption and hashtags and post from your phone at the suggested
   time.
4. **Comments/DMs:** paste one in and say "Draft a reply", then copy it back.

### Useful prompts

| Prompt | What you get |
|---|---|
| `Plan this week` | 7-day calendar |
| `Write a Reel: before/after of a 2BHK kitchen in MVP Colony` | Hook, script, on-screen text, caption, hashtags, CTA, posting time |
| `Carousel: laminate vs acrylic vs PU shutters` | 8–10 slide texts + caption |
| `YouTube video: Cost of a modular kitchen in Vizag 2026` | SEO title, description, tags, chapters, script, thumbnail text |
| `Check these photos for quality` + photos | Which are usable and which to reshoot |
| `Shot list for a factory process Reel` | What to film, shot by shot |
